How it works

Finding a home in Nairobi, in four simple steps.

{% endblock %} {% block content %}
1. Find a home

Type a place or building. Suggestions appear as you type, so you never need to spell it perfectly. Homes from several sites are shown together.

2. See the exact unit

Each result shows the building, the floor, the unit number and whether it is vacant, with its GPS spot on a map.

3. Check you can afford it

"What can I afford?" takes your income and workplace and lists the homes you can go for, with the real monthly cost including travel.

4. Reserve with M-Pesa

Pay a small deposit from your phone so the unit is held for you while you view it.

For renters
  1. Create a free account and choose Renter.
  2. Use Find a home to search by place, building, floor or price.
  3. Open What can I afford? to get a personal list.
  4. Press Save this search to be told when a matching home is added.
  5. Press Reserve with M-Pesa on a vacant unit.
For landlords
  1. Create an account and choose Landlord.
  2. Use Add listing. Pick the neighbourhood from the list.
  3. Enter the building, floor and unit number, and mark it vacant or occupied.
  4. Press Use my current location while you stand in the building to save its exact GPS point.
  5. See the deposits renters pay under Payments received.
Good to know
  • Where do the homes come from? Landlords add them here, and a scraper collects public adverts from other sites. Each scraped listing links back to the original advert.
  • Is the price fair? Each home is compared with the average rent of its neighbourhood. We only compare when at least 3 homes are known in the area.
  • "Approximate" location: when an advert gives no exact GPS point, we show the centre of the neighbourhood and say so.
  • Travel costs are estimates from typical Nairobi fares and traffic. They help you compare areas; they are not a route planner.
